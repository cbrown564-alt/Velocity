Pro reports locking the original source, public definitions and 32 prospective conditions before its first draw; a local snapshot was also saved before local execution. The 3,000 fictional PantryPace households each evaluate one randomly assigned monthly price (£4, £7, £10 or £13). Baseline weights are positive and unequal. Module exits, numeric item missingness and conditional routes remain explicit. The reference covers all five intent categories, valid-case and conservative all-assigned estimates, logical missing-data ranges, assigned balance, five prespecified Holm families, hypothetical cost scenarios, diagnostic correlations and suppressed premium-niche cells.
