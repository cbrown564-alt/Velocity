## Provenance

- **Dataset:** synthetic, seed `20260703`, generated by
  `scripts/python/generate_brand_tracker.py` + `brand_tracker_config.json`. The
  planted SCR storyline is auditable in the config and verified in the ground
  truth (`storyline_checks`). No real brand names or logos.
- **Build:** `VelocityEngine` (node runtime) → `buildDeck` → `exportDeck (pptx)`
  → `commitDeck`, mirroring the `scripts/agent-demo.ts` reference harness. Weight
  `wt` applied; the two attribute driver slides derive a Top-2-Box net via the
  shipped `recode` primitive (recipe step 6) so the 1–5 scale renders cleanly.
- **Numbers:** every action title's magnitude and significance verdict is drawn
  from `validation/brand_tracker_ground_truth.json` (`deltas.w3_w4`,
  `storyline_checks`). Significant movers are stated with direction + magnitude;
  the non-significant "worth the price" mover is titled *broadly stable*; the
  low-base Atlas NPS carries a directional caveat.
