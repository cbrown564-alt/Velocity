A decision note for the Meridian team

What we know

Unweighted preference rose from 24% to 30%. With the same population targets applied to both waves, preference is 26% in each wave.

The sample now contains more of the group with higher preference. Neither group’s observed preference rate changed.

What this means for the decision

Do not use the raw rise alone to justify more investment. Check commercial performance and campaign exposure before changing spend.

Still unanswered

Has brand trust improved? The wording changed between waves; this comparison cannot answer it yet.
