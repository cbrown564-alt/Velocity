# Design Iteration 03 — Slides 7–10

This iteration completes the back half of the Category Board storyboard using only evidence already validated in SBT-001.

## Slide 7 — Northstar divergence

**Headline:** `Northstar still looks healthy from the outside. Its customers tell a different story.`

The page is a deliberate market/customer juxtaposition, not another generic trend slide.

- left: prompted awareness remains ~94%;
- centre/right: current-customer NPS collapses in W4;
- supporting evidence: recent-problem incidence reaches 34.1% in W4;
- commentary synthesises the universes rather than collapsing them: the immediate vulnerability is customer-led while broad visibility remains intact.

Red is reserved for the experience deterioration, not used as a permanent Northstar brand colour.

## Slide 8 — Northstar recovery

**Headline:** `Wave 5 is better. It is not a return to the pre-shock position.`

Use W3 as the pre-incident anchor and compare W3 / W4 / W5 for two independently validated customer measures:

- NPS: approximately -12.5 → -60.7 → -33.2;
- recent problem incidence: 13.8% → 34.1% → 21.8%.

The visual argument is recovery-versus-restoration. W5 receives an amber/recovery treatment rather than green 'problem solved' treatment.

Do not add satisfaction/reliability merely to create a four-KPI dashboard unless those measures materially change the interpretation.

## Slide 9 — Harbour diagnosis

**Headline:** `The total market looks steady. Younger consumers are quietly leaving the consideration set.`

Use two trends on the **same scale**:

- total Harbour consideration remains around the mid-20s;
- 18–34 consideration falls from ~22% to ~15%.

This replaces the earlier unverified 'installed base' visual claim with a directly supported contrast: aggregate stability is masking youth erosion.

**Interpretation:** `Harbour is not collapsing today; it is becoming harder to replenish tomorrow.`

## Slide 10 — Implications

Dark editorial close. No recommendation cards and no generic 'strategic priorities'.

Headline: `Three questions should shape the next wave.`

Questions:

1. **Pulse:** Can younger-consumer consideration convert before campaign salience fades?
2. **Northstar:** Is W5 recovery continuing, or has advocacy settled at a weaker level?
3. **Harbour:** Can the brand rebuild younger-consumer relevance before the gap becomes structural?

The output remains appropriately research-led: these are questions implied by evidence, not prescriptions masquerading as survey findings.

## Accuracy correction carried forward

The prior Slide 6 conversion mockup used placeholder preference/usage values. Those values are **not benchmark gold** and must not appear in the final canonical deck until recomputed from the deterministic generator/reference layer. This design iteration therefore does not depend on them.
