# Design Iteration 02 — Slide 2 Selection

## Decision

Use **Alternative A — Editorial Synopsis** as the canonical design direction for Slide 2.

## Why it wins

- establishes one strong executive thesis before exposing supporting evidence;
- the evidence strip reads as one editorial synopsis rather than three dashboard cards;
- preserves equal evidential credibility for Pulse, Northstar and Harbour without forcing identical visual containers;
- creates a clean transition from the category-level thesis on Slide 1 into the competitive landscape on Slide 3;
- supports publication-style hierarchy: proposition → evidence → interpretation;
- leaves enough whitespace for the page to feel edited rather than filled.

## Canonical composition

### Headline

**Three pressures are reshaping the category.**

### Dek

Pulse is winning consideration. Northstar's customers are recoiling. Harbour is losing the next generation.

### Evidence strip

Three horizontally arranged evidence anchors separated by fine rules, not boxes:

1. **Pulse** — `+6.9pp` consideration, W2→W3 — challenger breakout.
2. **Northstar** — `−48` NPS points, W3→W4 — customer shock hidden by continued market strength.
3. **Harbour** — `−7.0pp` 18–34 consideration, W1→W5 — slower erosion in future recruitment.

### Closing framing

A small subordinate line may label the three mechanisms:

`Acquisition · Experience · Renewal`

This should remain secondary; it is not a fourth headline.

## Refinements from the prototype

The selected prototype is a direction, not a frozen pixel layout. Before final deck generation:

- reduce the amount of uppercase labelling;
- ensure the three evidence anchors have enough horizontal breathing room;
- make the metric labels shorter and more legible;
- keep the explanatory sentences to one line/two short lines each;
- avoid making the evidence strip look like three implicit cards;
- use semantic colour only on brand label/key number, not whole sections;
- preserve a strong blank-space break between the dek and evidence strip.

## Implication for subsequent slides

Carry forward the **editorial hierarchy**, not the exact evidence-strip layout. Slides 3–10 should vary visual form according to their visual argument. Repetition of Slide 2's three-column structure would undermine the publication-quality goal.
