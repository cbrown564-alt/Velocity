# SBT-001 Design Iteration 01 — Slides 1–3

This iteration turns the merged storyboard/visual-argument work into concrete slide-design specifications before extending the deck.

## Shared visual system

- **Canvas:** 16:9, warm off-white/white rather than a dark corporate template.
- **Typography:** editorial display headline + neutral sans-serif body; headline must dominate before any chart is read.
- **Colour:** semantic only. Pulse teal, Northstar deep navy/red only for the CX shock, Harbour muted slate; Mosaic/Lumen contextual and subdued.
- **No dashboard chrome:** no repeated rounded cards, icon tiles or KPI containers.
- **Chart geometry:** charts must be built as continuous, inspectable paths or native charts; never assemble a line series from fragile disconnected decorative fragments.
- **Direct labelling:** labels sit beside marks/series where possible; legends are a fallback.
- **Commentary:** one synthesis sentence or short rail only where it adds interpretation beyond the graphic.
- **Footnotes:** universe/weight immediately visible but visually subordinate.

## Slide 1 — Category thesis

**Headline**  
The category is becoming more competitive.

**Deck/subhead**  
But the pressure is coming from three different directions.

**Composition**

- asymmetric editorial cover, not a dashboard;
- large two-line thesis in upper-left;
- three small narrative signposts across the lower third:
  - Pulse — acquisition momentum;
  - Northstar — customer vulnerability;
  - Harbour — generational erosion;
- signposts are text hierarchy, not three equal cards;
- substantial unused space is intentional.

**Pass condition:** a viewer can state the report thesis after looking for 3 seconds.

## Slide 2 — Executive synthesis

**Headline**  
Pulse is strengthening. Northstar is vulnerable beneath the surface. Harbour is weakening with future customers.

**Design**

One integrated vertical narrative with three rows, not three mini-slides.

Each row has:

1. a small sequence number / brand label;
2. one dominant piece of quantitative evidence;
3. the metric/universe in subordinate type;
4. one interpretation sentence.

Evidence anchors:

- Pulse: consideration movement;
- Northstar: W3→W4 NPS collapse;
- Harbour: W1→W5 youth-consideration erosion.

Rows differ subtly in spacing/emphasis so they do not read as generic cards. Pulse receives slightly more emphasis because it leads into the next chapter.

**Closing synthesis line:** competitive pressure is entering through acquisition, experience and generational renewal.

**Reject:** KPI-card layout, three identical coloured boxes, or a questionnaire-summary table.

## Slide 3 — Position × momentum

**Headline**  
Northstar owns the position. Pulse owns the momentum.

**Visual argument**

Scatter/position-momentum map:

- x = W5 total-sample consideration;
- y = W2→W5 change in percentage points;
- horizontal zero-change reference;
- every brand directly labelled;
- Pulse visually dominant;
- Northstar second-order anchor;
- Mosaic/Lumen/Harbour muted.

The plot must answer two questions simultaneously:

- who is competitively strong now?
- who is moving?

**Right-side commentary rail:** two short notes only:

- `POSITION — Northstar remains the strongest consideration brand but is not gaining ground.`
- `MOMENTUM — Pulse has moved furthest since W2; rank alone hides that story.`

**Reject:** five separate line charts, legend-dependent colour decoding, oversized quadrant backgrounds, decorative arrows or a paragraph explaining every brand.

## Render QA before slide 4

The first three slides must be rendered and inspected at both full-slide and thumbnail scale.

Required checks:

- headline remains dominant at thumbnail scale;
- no text wraps unexpectedly;
- plot labels do not collide;
- zero line is visually subordinate to data;
- direct labels remain readable;
- no connector/line discontinuities;
- page 2 reads as one argument rather than three cards;
- page 1 has enough whitespace to feel intentional rather than unfinished;
- typography, margins and footnote position are stable enough to propagate.

Do not extend the visual system to slides 4–10 until these checks pass.
