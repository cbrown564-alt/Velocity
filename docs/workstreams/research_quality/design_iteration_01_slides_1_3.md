# SBT-001 Design Iteration 01 — Slides 1–3

This specification reflects the accepted Alternative A opening direction plus the subsequent critical-review refinements. Slides 1–3 have deliberately different jobs: thesis → evidence-backed synopsis → analytical landscape.

## Shared visual system

- **Canvas:** 16:9, warm off-white/white rather than a dark corporate template.
- **Typography:** editorial display headline + neutral sans-serif body. As a render-tested starting point use approximately 34–38 pt finding headlines, 18–20 pt explanatory text, 16–18 pt chart labels and 12–14 pt source/base notes. Rewrite before shrinking.
- **Colour:** semantic only. Pulse teal; Northstar neutral/dark with red reserved for deterioration; Harbour muted slate; Mosaic/Lumen contextual and subdued. Do not rely on colour alone.
- **No dashboard chrome:** no repeated rounded cards, icon tiles or KPI containers.
- **Chart geometry:** continuous inspectable paths or native charts; never assemble a line series from fragile disconnected decorative fragments.
- **Direct labelling:** labels sit beside marks/series where possible; legends are a fallback.
- **Commentary:** synthesis only where it adds interpretation beyond the graphic.
- **Footnotes:** universe, weighting and period immediately visible when they materially change interpretation.
- **Numerical language:** distinguish percentages, percentage-point changes and NPS points.

## Slide 1 — Category thesis

**Headline**  
Pulse advances; Northstar and Harbour face different vulnerabilities.

**Context line**  
SBT-001 synthetic UK mobile brand tracker · Waves 1–5

**Composition**

- asymmetric editorial cover, not a dashboard;
- large concise thesis in upper-left;
- substantial unused space is intentional;
- do **not** repeat the three quantitative evidence anchors or recreate Slide 2 in the lower third;
- if brand signposts are retained, keep them extremely subordinate and non-quantitative.

**Pass condition:** a viewer understands the category-level proposition and the synthetic-study context within three seconds.

## Slide 2 — Executive synthesis

Alternative A remains canonical.

**Headline**  
Three pressures are reshaping the category.

**Dek**  
Pulse gains consideration. Northstar's customer advocacy falls sharply. Harbour weakens among 18–34s.

**Design**

Use one integrated editorial evidence strip, separated by whitespace/fine rules rather than cards.

Each evidence anchor contains:

1. brand label;
2. one dominant quantitative anchor;
3. metric + population + period immediately beneath it;
4. one short interpretation/qualification.

Canonical anchors:

- **Pulse:** `+6.9pp` — total-sample consideration, W2→W3; challenger breakout.
- **Northstar:** `−48 NPS points` — current customers, W3→W4; customer shock, with a short note that W5 partially recovers but remains below W3.
- **Harbour:** `−7.0pp` — consideration among 18–34s, W1→W5; younger-audience erosion.

These magnitudes are not directly comparable. Do not encode their relative sizes as bars/areas or imply a ranking across unlike measures.

**Closing synthesis line:** acquisition momentum, customer vulnerability and younger-audience erosion are distinct competitive pressures.

**Reject:** KPI-card layout, three identical coloured boxes, questionnaire-summary table, or tiny universe/period labels that disappear beneath the hero numbers.

## Slide 3 — Position × momentum

**Headline**  
Northstar leads on consideration; Pulse records the largest gain.

**Visual argument**

Scatter/position-momentum map:

- x = W5 total-sample consideration (%);
- y = W2→W5 change in consideration (percentage points);
- horizontal zero-change reference labelled `No change since W2`;
- every brand directly labelled;
- equal-sized points unless a third variable is explicitly encoded;
- Pulse highlighted through accent and annotation;
- Northstar second-order contextual anchor;
- Mosaic/Lumen/Harbour muted.

Do not add an arbitrary vertical threshold or quadrant shading. Current consideration and change are continuous measures; only the zero-change reference has an intrinsic boundary here.

**Commentary:** keep to one short synthesis if needed: `Current consideration and change answer different competitive questions.` The plot should do most of the explanatory work.

**Reject:** five separate line charts, legend-dependent decoding, oversized quadrant backgrounds, decorative arrows, unexplained bubble sizes or a paragraph explaining every brand.

## Render QA before slide 4

Required checks:

- headline remains dominant at thumbnail scale;
- no text wraps unexpectedly;
- Slide 1 does not duplicate Slide 2;
- Slide 2 metric/population/period labels remain readable and its unlike numbers do not look quantitatively comparable;
- Slide 3 plot labels do not collide;
- Slide 3 axes clearly distinguish % from percentage points;
- zero line is subordinate to data but visible;
- direct labels remain readable;
- no connector/line discontinuities;
- typography, margins and footnote position are stable enough to propagate.
