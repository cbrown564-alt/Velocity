# Design Iteration 05 — Full-Deck Integration QA

## Status

The 10-slide Category Board design is analytically unblocked and the structured storyboard/visual-argument contract have now been updated to match the accepted evidence corrections and critical review. The canonical sequence remains unchanged.

4. Pulse breakout

6. Pulse parallel-measure change

## Evidence and framing corrections

### Slide 6

All four measures use deterministic weighted values:

- prompted awareness: 57.79% → 73.82% (+16.03pp);
- total-sample consideration: 20.29% → 29.59% (+9.30pp);
- preference: 15.34% → 25.11% (+9.77pp);
- current provider: 15.16% → 19.79% (+4.63pp).

Innovation is removed because it uses a familiar-brand routed universe. The visual is no longer specified as a funnel/conversion ladder: these are four separate population measures, not observed respondent-level transitions. Use aligned W2/W5 dumbbells plus a prominent percentage-point change column. Do not use tapered funnel geometry, flow arrows or drop-off rates.

### Slide 8

Use only the two consequential current-customer measures: NPS and recent-problem incidence. Show W3/W4/W5 and make the W5-versus-W3 residual gap explicit. W3 is a pre-shock reference, not a demonstrated healthy benchmark.

### Slide 9

Use aggregate consideration versus 18–34 consideration on the same scale. Do not use the earlier installed-base framing or language implying that individual younger respondents are observed leaving the consideration set.

## Whole-deck narrative QA

### Opening

Slides 1–3 progressively narrow:

- Slide 1: concise category thesis plus synthetic-study identification; do not repeat Slide 2's three evidence anchors.
- Slide 2: evidence-backed synopsis using Alternative A; every anchor carries its metric, universe and period. Unlike magnitudes are not visually ranked. Northstar's W5 partial recovery is acknowledged locally.
- Slide 3: category structure through current consideration versus W2→W5 percentage-point change.

Do not reintroduce card-based executive-summary layouts.

### Pulse chapter

Slides 4–6 form one argument:

1. **breakout** — a pronounced W3 consideration increase is followed by higher W4/W5 readings;
2. **audience** — the observed W2→W3 increase is larger among 18–34s than 35+ respondents;
3. **measure gap** — consideration/preference gains exceed the gain in current-provider share.

Avoid causal campaign language. On Slide 5, do not claim that younger respondents account for most total-market growth unless contribution is separately established. On Slide 6, do not imply a respondent-level conversion funnel.

### Northstar chapter

Slides 7–8 intentionally change rhythm:

- Slide 7 juxtaposes high prompted awareness with current-customer NPS/problem deterioration. Awareness is not labelled as overall brand health and there is no prediction that awareness will later 'absorb' the shock.
- Slide 8 shows partial recovery and the remaining distance from W3 on NPS and problem incidence only.

Red means deterioration/event shock, not permanent Northstar identity. W5 recovery uses amber/neutral rather than green-success semantics.

### Harbour chapter

Slide 9 should feel quieter than the Pulse/Northstar pages. Use one directly labelled two-series line chart on a common scale where practical. The claim is aggregate/subgroup divergence over five waves, not individual departure, installed-base causation or a forecast beyond W5.

### Close

Slide 10 asks three operational questions and names the next-wave evidence that would answer them. It does not assume campaign salience will fade, that Northstar advocacy is structurally impaired, or that Harbour's younger-audience gap will necessarily become structural.

## Visual rhythm QA

The deck alternates page jobs rather than repeating one template:

- thesis / typography;
- editorial evidence strip;
- analytical map;
- hero trend;
- dumbbell + change comparison;
- aligned measure changes;
- juxtaposition;
- reference/recovery comparison;
- directly labelled paired trend;
- typography-led close.

Consistency comes from typography, margins, annotation, numerical conventions and source notes—not identical composition.

## Shared implementation rules for the next render

These are starting settings and must be verified in the actual PowerPoint render rather than treated as absolute requirements:

- finding headlines: approximately 34–38 pt, normally no more than two lines;
- explanatory/dek text: approximately 18–20 pt;
- chart labels: approximately 16–18 pt where space permits; remove excess labels before shrinking type;
- source/base notes: approximately 12–14 pt and readable in presentation context;
- rewrite an overlong headline rather than squeezing it typographically;
- give the dominant evidence graphic most of the usable body area on analytical slides;
- use direct labels by default and legends only when direct labels materially reduce clarity;
- use open/filled markers, labels or line treatment so colour is not the only wave/semantic cue;
- Pulse accent remains stable; red is reserved for deterioration/risk; amber/neutral indicates partial recovery;
- distinguish percentages, percentage-point changes and NPS points explicitly;
- calculate displayed changes from unrounded estimates, then round consistently;
- put universe, weighting and period beside the graphic when they change interpretation;
- avoid arbitrary quadrants, decorative arrows, unexplained bubble sizes and ornamental containers.

## Slide-specific chart configuration

### Slide 3

- x-axis = W5 total-sample consideration (%);
- y-axis = W2→W5 change (percentage points);
- visible zero-change reference;
- equal point size unless a third variable is explicitly encoded;

- make W2→W3 weighted change and W5 endpoint the main annotations;

- market awareness is a smaller counterpoint;
- `All eligible respondents` and `Current Northstar customers` labels sit adjacent to their measures;

### Slide 8

- show W3/W4/W5 plus explicit W5-versus-W3 residual gap;
- keep units/scales separate;

### Slide 9

## Final render checklist

Before treating a generated PPTX as the canonical E6 reference:

- render all 10 slides to images and PDF;
- inspect at full size and thumbnail/contact-sheet scale;
- verify no connector discontinuities, clipping or overlap;
- verify headline hierarchy at thumbnail scale;
- verify Slide 3 labels do not collide and axes distinguish % from percentage points;
- verify Slide 4 line path is continuous and campaign annotation is observational;
- verify Slide 5 dumbbell endpoints/change labels remain legible;
- verify Slide 6 cannot be read as a respondent-level funnel;
- verify Slide 7 universe labels are adjacent and unlike measures do not share an axis;
- verify Slide 8 residual gaps are immediately visible;
- verify Slide 9 line paths are continuous, directly labelled and share one scale;
- verify all displayed values against deterministic analysis/generator outputs;
- verify footnotes accurately state universe/weight/period;
- verify no low-base Mosaic material appears;
- verify the deck reads as one argument without speaker narration.

## Canonical artifact requirement

The final reviewable E6 reference should retain the assembled PPTX, an exported PDF and a contact sheet/per-slide renders alongside a clearly identified build/version. Contact-sheet review tests pacing and hierarchy; full-size review tests labels, annotations and geometry.
